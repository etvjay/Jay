Portfolio logic

The project is the system. The research isolates what the system taught me.

The case studies stay product-first: what exists, why it was built, what I contributed and what evidence supports it. When implementation pressure produces a more reusable systems question, that mechanism moves into the research archive.

OpenRails, for example, links outward to Workspace, Path, Pact, RailsCard, 2D Nonce Lanes and Dynamic Authorization Lanes without making the project page itself read like a paper.
