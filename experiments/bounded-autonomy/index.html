Bounded Autonomy

What is the smallest authority an autonomous actor needs to complete an economic objective?

01 / Testable hypothesis

Useful autonomous execution does not require unrestricted possession of the underlying authority.

The hypothesis predicts a middle regime between per-action human approval and full custody: authority can persist across actions while remaining scoped by capability, amount, frequency, time, resource, dependency and revocation.

04 / Current result

Authority can persist longer than one action while remaining narrower than ownership.

The useful design variable is not “human versus autonomous.” It is how much authority survives between actions, under which conditions, and with what blast radius.

05 / What it does not establish

The experiment does not show that bounded delegation is appropriate for every objective. Some tasks may require interactive approval; others may require stronger account-level controls than an application Runtime can provide.

It also does not claim that OpenRails Path replaces ERC-20 allowances, smart-account validation or wallet security.
