Evidence & Understanding

How far may a computational system legitimately move from what it observed toward what it claims to understand?

01 / Testable hypothesis

Derived economic meaning should never claim stronger evidence than the material that produced it.

If the hypothesis is correct, partial, stale or conflicting evidence should visibly constrain the strength of the resulting claim or judgment instead of disappearing during interpretation.

03 / Run ledger

The test is whether epistemic limits survive transformation.

04 / Current result

State, evidence, claim and judgment behave better as separate layers.

The strongest current result is negative discipline: the system can preserve cases where there is not enough evidence to justify a stronger conclusion.

05 / What it does not establish

Noema is still foundational rather than a live autonomous intelligence product. The experiment does not establish that every domain needs the same schema, or that explicit evidence objects always outperform simpler application logic.
