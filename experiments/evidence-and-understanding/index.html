Evidence & Understanding

How far may a computational system legitimately move from what it observed toward what it claims to understand?

HypothesisApparatusVariablesEvidenceCurrent resultFalsification
01 / Hypothesis

Derived economic meaning should carry an explicit evidence ceiling.

A system should not present a stronger claim than the evidence beneath it can support.

02 / Systems under test
Noema ↗

Tests evidence-bounded economic objects, canonical roots, freshness, provenance and verification state.

RJP ↗

Tests the transition from observed onchain behavior to bounded judgment and downstream consumption.

source reliability

04 / Evidence

Noema currently establishes the schema/canonicalization/verification foundation. RJP establishes a live path from reproducible evidence to revisioned judgment and mirrored consumption.

05 / Current result

State, evidence, claim and judgment remain more useful when they are not collapsed into one object.

06 / What would falsify it

If downstream systems can preserve equivalent provenance, freshness and claim discipline without carrying these distinctions, the additional evidence-state model would be unnecessary.
