Behavioural Identity

What does observable behaviour actually allow another system to conclude about an actor?

01 / Testable hypothesis

Useful behavioral judgments can remain domain-scoped without collapsing into a universal reputation score.

If the hypothesis is right, the same subject may legitimately receive different conclusions under different domains, evidence windows or consumer policies without inconsistency.

04 / Current result

The strongest current evidence favors bounded judgments over global scores.

The same observed actor can remain one identifier while meaning varies by domain, evidence window and downstream policy.

05 / What it does not establish

This experiment does not claim that domain-scoped judgment is always superior, that identity is reducible to onchain behavior, or that observed behavior exhausts what another actor may need to know.

A simpler score would falsify the need for this structure if it could preserve the same evidence, freshness, revision and policy independence with less complexity.
