About / Research practice

The unit
of work is
a question.

I use working systems to test claims about coordination, authority, evidence, autonomy and programmable economic relationships.

A result belongs in this archive when the mechanism can be inspected and the boundary of the claim is explicit.

Position

Code becomes the implementation of an argument.

Projects are not the primary unit of this practice. They are experimental apparatus used to pressure a research question until the assumptions, mechanisms and failure modes become clearer.

I am particularly interested in systems that must coordinate without collapsing meaningful boundaries: authority without unrestricted custody, evidence without inflated certainty, memory without surrendering present control, privacy without losing joint action, and composition without homogenization.

That is why the portfolio separates research, experiments and projects. The research states the question. Experiments test a mechanism. Projects are where those mechanisms meet real implementation constraints.

Editorial rule

Difference is not automatically improvement.

Research stories compare a construction against the actual substrate and established alternatives. If Ethereum, the EVM, ERC-4337, GenLayer, Flare or another system already provides a related mechanism, that lineage should be explained before claiming novelty.

The aim is not to make every primitive sound superior. It is to identify where the construction is useful, what it costs, which assumptions it introduces, and what remains unproven.
