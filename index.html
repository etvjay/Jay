Featured research 02 / RailsCard

Dynamic Authorization Lanes

When a nonce lane stops meaning “execution queue” and starts identifying one independently exercisable authorization.

Deferred RailsCards exposed a specific concurrency failure. The resulting design allocates a fresh replay domain to each outstanding authorization.
