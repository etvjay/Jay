Research directions

The projects are artifacts of continuing technical questions.

01

Programmable economic relationships

OpenRails · Concord · NOX ACCORD

What should exist between agreement and transaction? I explore economic relationships as persistent programmable objects: authority, terms, proof, exposure, settlement, receipts, and lifecycle remain connected rather than flattening into isolated transfers.

02

Evidence, judgment & economic understanding

RJP · Noema

How should software move from observing reality to acting on an interpretation of it? Raw state is not knowledge; evidence is not judgment; judgment is not authority. These systems preserve those boundaries.

03

Memory & autonomous agency

Engram · Thinking Reed

How can autonomous systems improve from experience without silently surrendering decision authority to memory? The focus is causal influence, provenance, bounded execution, and persistent continuity.

04

Confidential & bounded coordination

NOX ACCORD · Concord

What should remain private, what must become verifiable, and where should trust reside? Confidentiality is treated as a coordination primitive, not merely a data-hiding feature.

Research position

Builds are arguments that have survived implementation.

The portfolio deliberately distinguishes implemented, tested, simulated, blocked, testnet, foundation, and research states. Code is evidence of an argument; deployment is evidence of execution; neither is evidence of claims the system has not actually proven.

Working principles

Authority and evidence remain explicit.

Preserve boundaries.

Observation, inference, judgment, authorization, execution, and settlement are different system responsibilities.

Make failure useful.

Experiments are valuable when their limits are recorded and later systems can inherit the lesson rather than repeat the assumption.

Prove what changed.

Successful output is not enough. The useful question is what caused the result, what authority it exercised, and what evidence remains.
