Research is organized as stories, experiments and primitives. Projects are the apparatus used to test them—not the organizing unit.

Each note aims to establish the existing substrate first, then the implementation pressure, the mechanism we tried, what it enabled, what it cost, and what remains unproven.

Independent ordering domains for application-level authorizations.

Ethereum transaction nonces, ERC-4337 keyed nonces, and OpenRails’ account × channel model—compared without pretending that different semantics imply a universally better nonce architecture.

Featured research 02 / Deferred authority

Dynamic Authorization Lanes

Fresh replay domains for independently exercisable RailsCards.

The lane represents one deferred authorization rather than a long-lived queue. The mechanism emerged because two valid pre-signed cards could otherwise invalidate one another.
