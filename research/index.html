Questions before products.

Research is organized as experiments, primitives, and technical notes. Projects are the apparatus used to test them—not the organizing unit.

01

Governed Value & Programmable Relationships ↗

OpenRailsConcordOTC Chronograph

What changes when value moves according to the state and authority of a relationship rather than as isolated transactions?

The first code-first note is live: Workspace, Path and Pact establish the initial construction. Follow-on notes will cover RailsCard, dynamic and bilateral nonce lanes, Gaia, Root/Child Accord, and conditional capital DAGs.

02

Confidential & Bounded Coordination

NOX ACCORDConcord

Can independent actors coordinate private economic intent without surrendering authority or revealing every input?

Confidentiality is most useful when it changes what independent actors can safely coordinate—not merely when it hides data. The research moves from private comparison and bounded allocation toward persistent confidential relationships.

03

Evidence, Judgment & Economic Understanding

RJPNoema

How should software move from observing reality to acting on an interpretation of it?

Observation, evidence, inference, judgment, and authority are different objects. The goal is to preserve those distinctions from source material through economic understanding and downstream action.

04

Memory & Autonomous Agency

EngramThinking Reed

How can autonomous systems improve from experience without silently surrendering decision authority to memory?

Memory is operational only when influence can be reconstructed. The broader research asks how persistent human–AI systems retain uncertainty, evidence, dissent, authority, execution history, and revision over time.
