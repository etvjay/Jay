Thinking Reed

A persistent human–AI cognition and production system connecting thought, evidence, decisions, execution, and observed reality.

Research questionPositionExperimentBuildEvidenceBoundaryLineage

Research question

Can a human and AI work continuously across research and production without generated artifacts erasing uncertainty, dissent, authority, or the lineage of why something was built?

Position

Thoughts, observations, source assertions, inferences, assumptions, hypotheses, decisions, runs, artifacts, and evidence are not interchangeable. Persistent collaboration requires preserving those distinctions and tracing consequential execution back to the causes and permissions that produced it.

Experiment

Define a Reed Kernel, Thought Ledger, Evidence Engine, Council, agency fabric, specialized Foundries, verification fabric, maturity model, and continuity graph around a single loop from uncertain thought through experiment and artifact to observed reality and revision.

Build

thought → articulation → investigation → deliberation → specification → experiment → artifact → system → reality → observation → revision.

Thinking Reed is being implemented as a system that contracts and composes specialized Foundries—Research, Opportunity, Experiment, Product, Systems, Interface, Sound, Demo, Learning—rather than collapsing them into one universal agent.

Evidence

  • Constitutional object distinctions and authority rules defined.
  • Foundry maturity model and shared production standard established.
  • Program decomposition spans kernel, evidence, council, agency, verification, continuity, and operations.
  • First implementation target is a CLI + REST + PostgreSQL-backed vertical with persisted lineage.

Current boundary

Thinking Reed is an active research system, not a production platform. Its architecture and implementation direction are presented without implying F7 or production maturity for the full Foundry system.

Research lineage

thought → evidence → decision → bounded execution → artifact → observed reality → revision
