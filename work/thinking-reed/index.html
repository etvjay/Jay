Thinking Reed

A persistent human–AI cognition and production system that keeps thought, evidence, uncertainty, decisions, execution, and observed reality connected.

Research questionPositionExperimentBuildEvidenceBoundaryLineageSource

Research question

Can a human and AI work continuously across research and production without generated artifacts erasing uncertainty, dissent, authority, or the lineage of why something was built?

Working position

Thoughts, observations, assertions, assumptions, hypotheses, decisions, runs, artifacts, and evidence should remain distinct first-class records connected through a persistent continuity graph.

Experiment

Define a Reed Kernel, Thought Ledger, Evidence Engine, Council, capability-routing fabric, specialized Foundries, verification fabric, and continuity model around the loop thought → investigation → decision → experiment → artifact → reality → observation → revision.

Build

Thinking Reed is being implemented as the core system that contracts and composes specialized Foundries rather than silently collapsing them into one general agent.

Evidence

  • Constitutional object distinctions and authority rules defined.
  • Foundry maturity model and shared production standard defined.
  • Program decomposition established across kernel, evidence, council, agency, verification, continuity, and operations.
  • First vertical targets CLI + REST + PostgreSQL-backed persistent lineage.

Current boundary

Thinking Reed is an active research system, not a production platform. The portfolio presents its architecture and implementation direction without implying completed F7/production maturity.

Research lineage

Thought → evidence → decision → bounded execution → artifact → observed reality → revision.
