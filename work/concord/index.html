Concord

A confidential programmable relationship system for coordinating capital between independent parties.

Research questionPositionExperimentBuildEvidenceBoundaryLineage

Research question

Can independent capital providers coordinate private terms inside one persistent economic relationship without erasing provider-level authority, exposure, and settlement lineage?

Position

The durable object should be the relationship itself. Confidential formation can happen inside a bounded session while public execution retains provider-specific commitments, exposure, draws, repayment, and lineage.

Experiment

Combine a Root Accord, bounded Makkari FCC session, deterministic CoFill allocation, Child Accords, provider funding, multi-child draws, repayment, and restored capacity while keeping confidential formation separate from public economic truth.

Build

Root Accord → Makkari session → CoFill allocation → Child Accords → funded capacity → draws → repayment.

Concord implements Solidity relationship and facility contracts, a Go FCC extension and allocator, a recorded Coston2 lifecycle, frontend evidence surfaces, and shared REST, SDK, MCP, and runtime boundaries.

Evidence

  • Recorded Coston2 Root Accord and Child Accord lifecycle.
  • Provider funding, multi-child draw legs, repayment, and restored-capacity evidence.
  • Go FCC extension using encrypted instruction handling and deterministic CoFill allocation.
  • Public demo, evidence ledger, and reproducibility runbooks in the repository.

Current boundary

The development confidentiality path may use a simulated TEE; Concord does not claim hardware-backed production confidentiality. Public settlement, exposure, accepted capacity, and relationship lineage remain onchain.

Research lineage

confidential formation + persistent economic relationship → provider-specific exposure → verifiable settlement lineage
