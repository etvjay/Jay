Research question

What should exist between an agreement and a transaction?

Position

Commercial coordination is more legible when authority, accepted terms, proof, settlement, and receipts remain part of one durable lifecycle. A payment should be the consequence of a governed relationship, not the only object the system remembers.

Experiment

Model a persistent Workspace where people, businesses, applications, and agents operate through bounded Paths; turn requests into Intents and Proposals; bind accepted terms in a Pact; verify Proof; settle direct or streamed USDC on Arc; and reconcile the result through a Receipt.

Build

Workspace → delegated authority → agreement → proof → settlement → receipt.

OpenRails implements the coordination runtime, Arc settlement contracts, wallet-signed state transitions, relayed execution, indexed reads, and shared SDK, CLI, REST, and MCP surfaces. Circle infrastructure is integrated around the lifecycle rather than replacing its authority model.

Evidence

  • Deployed V2 contract system on Arc with USDC-native settlement.
  • Persistent public Runtime for Workspace coordination.
  • EOA and EIP-1271 signing paths, replay protection, escrow, streaming settlement, and receipts.
  • Published SDK and MCP integration surfaces.
  • Fresh public Workspace-to-settlement verification records documented in the repository.

Current boundary

OpenRails is the coordination, clearing, and settlement layer—not a wallet, bridge, or autonomous financial authority. Wallets and applications authorize execution; Arc transaction evidence and Vault state remain the source of truth for value movement.

Research lineage

payment → intent → bounded authority → agreement + proof → persistent commercial relationship → verifiable settlement
